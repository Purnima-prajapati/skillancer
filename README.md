# Freelancing-website
SkilLancer is a user-friendly freelancing platform that connects clients with freelancers.
<br>Clients can post jobs, browse freelancer profiles, and communicate via secure messaging. Freelancers can showcase their skills, portfolios, and collaborate with clients. 
<br>The platform also offers project management tools, and a rating system for transparency and trust. Simplify your freelancing experience with skilLancer.
<br>It also includes the Azure service like monitering ,anlytics and hosting,etc.
